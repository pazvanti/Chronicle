Distraction-Free Chapter Writing & Dual-View Notes

A focused writing canvas built for authors. Organize chapters with smooth drag-and-drop, leave inline feedback with a 6-color highlight palette, split chapters at the cursor in one click, and clean up punctuation automatically.

  • Instant Markdown & EPUB Manuscript Import: Drag and drop any .md, .markdown, or .epub file. Markdown files are automatically split into structured chapters on top-level headings (#), with YAML frontmatter parsing, formatted tables, and images preserved.
  • Version Snapshots & Visual Diff Studio: Capture frozen restore points of your manuscript before daring revisions. Compare any past snapshot against your active draft in a Git-style side-by-side view, and restore chapters, paragraphs, or single words with 1 click.
  • Typewriter Zen Mode & Focus Dimming: Lock your cursor vertically centered at natural eye level while text flows upwards beneath it. Inactive paragraphs softly dim into 35% shadow, and the zero-UI Ghost HUD only appears when your mouse moves.
  • Dual-View Author Comments: Highlight any text to drop margin notes. Jump to notes instantly and toggle annotations on or off when you want pure focus.
  • Automated Typography Cleanup: Converts straight quotes to smart curly quotes, fixes em-dashes and ellipses, and cleans up accidental double spaces.
  • Zero-Leak Export Guarantee: Draft comments and work notes are automatically stripped before PDF and EPUB export so published books stay clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from independent novelists and authors about Chronicle.

Yes. Chronicle is completely free and open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPLv3). There are no subscriptions, no locked tiers, no ads, and no hidden paywalls. You retain 100% intellectual property ownership over everything you write and export.

Chronicle is 100% GenAI-Free. There are no LLMs, no text generators, and no autocomplete replacing your writing. The only neural technology in Chronicle is the optional Kokoro voice synthesis engine, which runs 100% locally on your computer to read your own words aloud for proofreading cadence.

Zen Mode strips away all window chrome, sidebars, borders, and menus for complete distraction-free focus. It can switch on automatically the moment you start typing, featuring three essential authoring mechanics:

1. Typewriter Scrolling: Your typing line stays vertically locked in the exact center of your screen as text smoothly scrolls upwards underneath it, keeping your posture relaxed at eye level.
2. Paragraph Focus Dimming: The active paragraph where your cursor rests stays illuminated at 100% opacity while all surrounding text softly dims to 35%, silencing internal editing voices.
3. The Ghost HUD: Pure zero-UI while typing. The moment you move your mouse, a floating translucent glass capsule HUD softly appears with your live session word count (+X words today), chapter indicator, and a floating chapter menu to switch scenes without leaving Zen mode.

The Presence Grid is an intelligent chapter-by-chapter matrix that analyzes your manuscript to track where every character and location appears throughout your book. It renders interactive heatmaps showing mention frequencies, character density, and screen time balance.

It instantly alerts you if a key character mysteriously disappears for multiple chapters, and lets you click any cell to inspect in-text dialogue excerpts, jump straight into the scene, or export the entire cast tracking matrix to a spreadsheet for editorial analysis.

Chronicle features an integrated, Git-grade Version Snapshots & Comparison Studio built specifically for novelists:

1. Instant Project Snapshots: Take a complete, frozen milestone of your entire project (chapters, character sheets, timelines, locations, and metadata) in a single click before embarking on major structural edits or rewrites.
2. Side-by-Side Visual Comparison: Open any snapshot in a Git-like side-by-side diff studio. Your active draft is displayed on the left and the snapshot on the right, with color-coded additions in emerald, deletions in rose, and word-level changes highlighted.
3. 1-Click Paragraph & Word-Level Restore: Cherry-pick exactly what you want to bring back. Click [Restore] in the center gutter to restore an entire paragraph, or hover over any modified phrase to restore just a single word or sentence while leaving the rest of the chapter intact.
4. Selective Module Rollback: If you revised your manuscript but accidentally lost a character profile or complex timeline arc, selective restore allows you to recover only the Timelines or Character sheets from a snapshot without touching your manuscript text.

Chronicle is a first-class, cross-platform application natively available on Windows, macOS, Linux, and any modern Web Browser:

• Windows: Portable standalone executable (Chronicle.exe) for Windows 10 & 11 (64-bit). Direct run with zero installation or administrator privileges required.
• macOS: Standalone disk image (Chronicle.dmg) with native universal binary support for both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs.
• Linux: Portable universal AppImage (Chronicle.AppImage) that runs out of the box across Fedora, Ubuntu, Debian, Arch Linux, openSUSE, and other distributions. Native .deb and .rpm packages are also supported.
• In-Browser Studio: Runs entirely client-side in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge with private offline IndexedDB storage.
